## 终版高分范文

As is distinctly illustrated in the chart, the market share of domestic cars surged from about 25% in 2008 to nearly 32% in 2009, while that of Japanese brands slipped from 35% to 25%, surrendering the lead to domestic cars, whereas American brands held steady at 12%.

Two interrelated factors account for this dramatic surge. To begin with, the rapid development of China's automobile industry has sharply raised the manufacturing capability of domestic brands, thereby improving the quality of their products. More importantly, this progress has reshaped the mindset of Chinese consumers, who once dismissed domestic cars as a byword for inferior quality but now regard them as symbols of quality and modernity, so public trust keeps growing. Moreover, this rise is a microcosm of China's overall industrial progress, where both hard power and soft power have grown.

To consolidate this momentum, the government should continue to carry out sound policies, enterprises should keep sharpening their technological edge, and consumers should give more support to domestic brands. Only in this way can Chinese brands earn wider recognition.
