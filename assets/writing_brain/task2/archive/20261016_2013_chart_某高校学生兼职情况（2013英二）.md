## 终版高分范文

As is distinctly illustrated in the chart, the percentage of university students holding a part-time job climbed from 67.77% in the first year to 71.13% and 71.93% in the second and third years, before surging to 88.24% in the fourth year.

This steady rise results from the different situations students face at each stage of campus life. To begin with, the unfamiliar environment and heavy academic burden force freshmen to devote much time to adapting to the transition from high school, which leaves them little time for part-time work. When it comes to second- and third-year students, they have already adapted to campus life, so the figure rises accordingly. In the fourth year, their situation shifts again, as they are about to leave campus and live independently in society. Driven by the pressure of fierce employment competition, they take part-time jobs to accumulate work experience and achieve financial independence.

All in all, the rising percentage indicates that students attach greater importance to practical experience. Therefore, they should balance study and work, and universities should offer more targeted guidance.
