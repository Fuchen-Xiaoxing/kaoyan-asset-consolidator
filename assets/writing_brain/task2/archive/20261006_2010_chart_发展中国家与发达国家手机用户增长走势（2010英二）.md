## 终版高分范文

As is distinctly illustrated in the chart, mobile-phone subscriptions in developing countries surged dramatically from about 0.4 billion in 2000 to nearly 4 billion in 2008, while those in developed countries experienced a rather slow increase and stayed at about 1 billion.

A number of factors contribute to this sharp increase. First, with rapid economic development, developing countries have built many modern industries and factories.Industrial mass manufacturing has drastically brought down overhead costs, rendering mobile phones financially accessible to the public. Second, these countries have a huge population, and mobile communication has become a basic need in daily life.
Moreover, the rapid improvement of network coverage has made mobile services available even in remote areas. By contrast, the market in developed countries has been saturated for years, so the demand for new subscriptions is limited.

This trend shows that new technologies are spreading faster in developing countries, and it will bring more people into the digital world. Therefore, both governments and companies should work together to make mobile services cheaper and easier to use.
