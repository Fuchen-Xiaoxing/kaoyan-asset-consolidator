## 终版高分范文

As is distinctly illustrated in the pie chart, New Year gifts account for the largest share of residents' spending in a certain Chinese city during the Spring Festival holiday, at 40%, while transportation, meals and other items each make up 20%.

This pattern stems largely from the Spring Festival culture, whose core is family reunion. Gifts and meals together account for 60%, as both are ways of staying close to family and friends. After a year apart, people working in other cities head home to their parents, and transportation becomes an unavoidable cost. Besides, a carefully chosen gift conveys affection and gratitude better than any words. The reunion dinner, in particular, carries special cultural weight, for it is at the table that ties are renewed. Other customs such as setting off fireworks remain common.

Overall, the figures mirror Chinese people's festive habits and their deep affection for family and friends. Yet generosity should not turn into a contest of spending: what matters is sincerity, not a price tag. Only by spending sensibly can we keep the festival meaningful.
