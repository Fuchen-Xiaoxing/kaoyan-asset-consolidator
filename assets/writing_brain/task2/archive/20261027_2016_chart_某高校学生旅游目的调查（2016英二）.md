## 终版高分范文

As is distinctly illustrated in the chart, sightseeing accounts for the largest share of college students' travel purposes, at 37%, with pressure relief ranking second at 33%. Merely 9% and 6% travel to make friends or build independence, while the remaining 15% have other goals.

This distribution stems largely from two factors: the appeal of natural scenery and the weight of study. Sightseeing ranks first as China is home to rich tourist resources, ranging from historical sites such as the Great Wall to natural landscapes such as Mount Huangshan, and such scenery satisfies young people's curiosity to explore the world. Besides, students carry a heavy workload of coursework and exams, so a short trip has become a rare chance to relax and recharge. By contrast, merely 15% travel to make friends or build independence, revealing that most see travel as relaxation rather than growth.

Whatever their purposes, students benefit from a well-planned trip. A trip to relax is as valuable as one to learn. Most importantly, safety comes first, for a trip is rewarding only when students return home safe.
