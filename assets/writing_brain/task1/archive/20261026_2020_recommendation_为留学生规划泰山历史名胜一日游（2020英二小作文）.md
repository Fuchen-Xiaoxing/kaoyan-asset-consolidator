## 终版高分范文

Dear students,

    Knowing that you are curious about Chinese historical sites, I am writing to introduce my tour plan and give you some tips.

    Located in Shandong Province, Mount Tai is home to countless stone carvings, offering a fascinating glimpse into China's glorious past. It has witnessed dynasties rise and fall, and visitors ranging from emperors and poets to ordinary travellers have climbed it, so the trip will help you learn about Chinese history. As for tips, it is wise to pack warm clothes, for the temperature drops sharply at the summit. Besides, feel free to raise questions on the spot, for I will explain the history behind each carving.

    I hope this trip will deepen your understanding of Chinese culture and bring you unforgettable memories. Please contact me with any questions.

                                        Best wishes,
                                        Li Ming
