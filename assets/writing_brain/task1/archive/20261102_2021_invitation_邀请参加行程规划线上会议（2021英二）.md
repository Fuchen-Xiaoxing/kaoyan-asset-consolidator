Dear Jack,

    Knowing that you are eager to learn about Chinese history, I am writing to invite you to an online meeting at 7 p.m. this Sunday on Tencent Meeting, where we will plan our coming trip together.

    With the theme of "Where to Go", this meeting aims to settle our trip destination and expose you to fascinating historical sites, before our teacher helps us make the final decision. Given your keen interest in Chinese history, your suggestions would be highly valued at the meeting.

    The session will run for about two hours, and the meeting link will reach your mailbox in advance. We would be thrilled if you could accept our invitation. Looking forward to a lively discussion with you.

                                        Best regards,
                                        Li Ming
