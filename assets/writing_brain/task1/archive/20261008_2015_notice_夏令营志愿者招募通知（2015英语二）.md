Notice
                             May 10th, 2026

To help high school students gain exposure to university life, our university is scheduled to host a summer camp on June 20th, 2026. A wide variety of activities, including campus tours, lectures and discussions with undergraduates, will be offered.

In order to ensure the smooth running of the event, we are recruiting volunteers to assist in receiving and registering the visiting students, guiding them around the campus, maintaining order and answering their questions. Patience and effective communication skills are the qualities we value most in candidates.

Every volunteer will earn bonus points. Please do not hesitate to join us by submitting your application form to the Students' Union office by May 20th.

The Students' Union
