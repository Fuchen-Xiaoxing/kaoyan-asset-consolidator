Dear Professor Smith,

    Thank you for trusting me to arrange the debate. With everything ready except the topic, I am writing to share my suggestion and arrangements.

    Our debate could revolve around one question: should the city spend its limited funds on buses or subways first? This topic is debatable, for it involves two competing views on how to spend the funds: buses cost less but get stuck in traffic jams, while subways require huge investment and years to build.

    I will announce the topic this Friday, and the debate will be held in Room 301 of the library from 6 p.m. to 8 p.m. next Friday. It would be much appreciated if you could offer me some advice.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
