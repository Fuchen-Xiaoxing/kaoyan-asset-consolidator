## 终版高分范文

Dear Jack,

    Thank you for your warm congratulations on my winning the contest. Knowing how eager you are to improve your translation, I am writing to share a few tips.

    To begin with, expand your vocabulary by keeping a word list, for words are the building blocks of every sentence. Besides, analyze sentences by charting their trunks, which will help you make sense of long and complex sentences. Moreover, grasp the main idea first and translate in context rather than word by word, which will make your translation read naturally. Above all, put these methods into practice, for only practice can turn them into your own skills.

    I hope my suggestions would be beneficial to you. For any questions, please feel free to contact me.

                                        Best wishes,
                                        Li Ming
