## 终版高分范文

Dear Li Ming,

    Congratulations on your admission to such a prestigious university. I am writing to offer some suggestions on how to get prepared for university life.

    To begin with, you had better learn to manage your monthly budget by keeping a record of your spending, or you may run out of money before the month ends. Besides, you are encouraged to get along with your roommates, who will be your closest companions for the next four years. As for your studies, it is never too early to gain exposure to your major, which will undoubtedly help you navigate your career path.

    Anyway, I wish you a fulfilling and rewarding university life.

                                        Yours sincerely,
                                        Zhang Wei

### 结构与语言亮点复盘

- **正文 109 词**，段落视觉比例 2.2 : 6.9 : 0.9，贴合 2-6-2 黄金视觉律（首段轻、中段重、尾段收）。
- **首段**：祝贺句以 `such a prestigious university` 注入情感；目的句用 `I am writing to offer you some suggestions on how to get prepared…` 精准回扣题干的 "get prepared"。**最终定稿删去了分词状语共情句**，理由是零采分 + 与目的句功能重叠 + 篇幅经济（119 词降至 109 词）。
- **次段三条建议，动词承重、层级对齐**：
  1. 理财自理：`you had better learn to manage your monthly budget by keeping a record of your spending`，后接 **警示句** `or you may run out of money before the month ends`（用时间锚点替代模糊词 soon）；
  2. 人际自理：`you are encouraged to get along with your roommates, who will be your closest companions for the next four years`（建议 + 定语从句补理由）；
  3. 专业认知：`it is never too early to gain exposure to your major, which will undoubtedly help you navigate your career path`（双重否定式强调 + 两个外刊级动宾）。
- **尾段**：`Anyway + I wish you a fulfilling and rewarding university life`，温情收束，不重复祝贺（官方范文此处重复 congratulations，本篇择善而从，不复刻冗余）。
- **格式合规**：称呼顶格逗号、段首缩进、敬语与署名靠右、零口语缩写、零感叹号、零超纲词、零无中生有。

### 训练过程关键节点复盘

1. **初稿诊断（约 149 词，严重超载）**：确认三段结构与"首段祝贺 + 段末引出建议"的构思完全成立；指出初稿核心症结为 **"描述状态"而非"准备动作"**，并用**前置动词归位法**（learn to / get into / gain exposure to）把句子挂回"入学前此刻"。
2. **减法得分律实战**：学员原想"生活上再加一个场景"，私教判定为"不要加、要换"，给出三个候选动宾供其自选，最终锁定"管理生活费 + 与室友相处"两条同层级自理技能。
3. **多轮基础版基线迭代**：第一轮保留学员自写的 `Knowing that…` 分词状语并修正三处错误（I'm → I am、sharing you → I am writing to offer you、suggestion → suggestions）；第二轮按学员指令替换生活建议两条。
4. **真题身份核验的关键纠正**：学员提供的题目为 **2011 英语二**（非英语一），官方范文标尺显示题面指定署名为 **Zhang Wei**，据此更正了基础版误署的 Li Ming。
5. **高级版升华**：先调取 2011 英二官方范文标尺，执行防写偏五项自查（真题属性 / 语域标尺 / 细节边界 / 词汇适配 / 动词承重）；主动弃用语域错配的知识库公文型条目（T1_ADV_001 / 002 / TMP_001）。
6. **结语定稿决策**：学员就分词状语"是否有必要"提问，经字数与功能重叠的双重测算，最终选择**删除（方案 A）**，全篇降至 109 词。

### 题目原文 (Directions)

Suppose your cousin Li Ming has just been admitted to a university. Write him/her a letter to
1) congratulate him/her, and
2) give him/her suggestions on how to get prepared for university life.
You should write about 100 words on ANSWER SHEET 2.
Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Zhang Wei" instead.
Do not write the address. (10 points)
