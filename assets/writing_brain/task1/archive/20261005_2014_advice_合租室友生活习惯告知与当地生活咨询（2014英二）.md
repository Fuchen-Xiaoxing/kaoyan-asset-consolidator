## 终版高分范文

Dear John,

    I am delighted to be your roommate during my time abroad. Before moving in, I would like to brief you about my living habits so that we can get acquainted with each other.

    I keep early hours and never smoke, getting up at 8:00 a.m. and turning in around midnight. As for food, I stick to a light diet and often cook Chinese meals. Would you mind if I cooked in our kitchen occasionally?

    By the way, could you give me some tips on local social customs, the particular rules in our apartment and how to respect each other's privacy? Since you are a local, I am sure your advice will help me fit in with local life.

                                        Best wishes,
                                        Li Ming
