## 终版高分范文

Dear Professor Smith,

    Please accept my sincere apology for not being able to visit you as planned. I fully appreciate that a change at such short notice may disrupt your busy schedule, and I am truly sorry for the trouble it causes you.

    Having looked forward to our meeting for weeks, I woke up with a high fever this morning. After a clinic examination, I was diagnosed with acute influenza, and the physician advised me to take bed rest, leaving me little choice but to cancel the trip.

    Much to my regret, we cannot meet as scheduled. I was wondering whether we could rearrange the appointment at your earliest convenience. Please let me know when you will be available.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
