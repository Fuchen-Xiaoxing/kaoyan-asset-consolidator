## 终版高分范文

Dear classmates,

    Under our teachers' guidance, our class is scheduled to hold a charity sale for kids in need of help at 3 p.m. next Sunday in our classroom.

    All the funds raised will go directly to children on the verge of dropping out of school under financial constraints, helping them return to the classroom. A wide variety of items, from second-hand books to daily necessities, will be on sale at a discount. Our school and a local charity will also provide a starting fund.

    It is your participation that will bring these children one step closer to the classroom. You do not want to miss this chance, and a small free gift awaits every participant.

                                        Yours sincerely,
                                        Li Ming
