# 2012 英语二 · 投诉信（Complaint Email）

## 一、真题题干
Suppose you have found something wrong with the electronic dictionary that you bought from an online store the other day. Write an email to the customer service center to 1) make a complaint, and 2) demand a prompt solution.
（指定署名：Zhang Wei；受众权责：消费者对客服部门；语域：neutral_formal）

## 二、终版满分范文

Dear Sir or Madam,

    I am writing to lodge a formal complaint regarding the electronic dictionary that I purchased from your online store on September 20th.

    Having placed high trust in your brand, I was dismayed to discover that the device fails to function properly. It shuts down erratically and persistently, and its screen has now gone blank, which has severely disrupted my daily English study. Worse still, when I turned to your online customer service yesterday, the staff offered no constructive solution, which I find utterly unacceptable.

    Under these circumstances, I feel fully justified in requesting an immediate replacement with a brand-new device or a full refund within three business days. I would appreciate a prompt settlement of my case.

                                        Yours faithfully,
                                        Zhang Wei

## 三、本篇复盘沉淀

### 1. 初稿病灶清单（学生原文）
- 拼写：`expree` → `express`；`respose` → `response`
- 搭配：`On the other day` → `the other day`；`a electronic` → `an electronic`；`the online store` → `your online store`
- 从句：逗号 + `that` 引导定语从句（语法死伤，须改 `which`）
- 用词：`detained my study` → `delayed / disrupted my study`
- 谓语：`My needs is simple`（主谓不一致）；`change a new one to me`（change 不接双宾语）
- 语域：第一段 `your goods with low quality` 属无据指控；`give me a rapid response` 属命令语气
- 敬语：`Yours` 不完整，应为 `Yours faithfully,`

### 2. 采分点映射
- 采分点 1（交代网购电子词典信息并正式提出质量投诉）：第一段 `lodge a formal complaint regarding...` 一句四要素齐全
- 采分点 2（详细列举故障事实）：`shuts down erratically and persistently` + `screen has now gone blank` + `no constructive solution`（售后敷衍事实链）
- 采分点 3（明确合理诉求与解决期限）：`an immediate replacement...or a full refund within three business days`

### 3. 本篇高级句型
- 现在分词的完成式作原因状语：`Having placed high trust in your brand, I was dismayed to discover that...`
- `which` 引导非限制性定语从句（先行词为整个主句）：`..., which has severely disrupted my daily English study.` / `..., which I find utterly unacceptable.`
- 介词短语承重 + 名词化诉求句：`Under these circumstances, I feel fully justified in requesting...`
